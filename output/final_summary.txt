 Artificial Intelligence (AI) is currently driving a transformative wave across multiple sectors.
 By automating tasks and enhancing decision-making capabilities, it is reshaping fields such as healthcare, finance, and transportation.
 Central to this revolution is Machine Learning (ML), a subset of AI that trains models through data analysis without being explicitly programmed. The versatility of ML allows for its application in diverse areas, from diagnosing diseases to optimizing financial portfolios and improving the efficiency of transport systems.
